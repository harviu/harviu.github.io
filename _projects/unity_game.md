I took a game design course at my second year of graduate study at The Ohio State University. I was also taking Geometric Modeling and Real-time Rendering course at the same year. That was when I started to get interested in computer graphics. 

I made a simple 3D game with Unity as the final project of the game design course. The game is called !EMALB, which is "BLAME!" spelled backwards. The game is inspired by the manga "BLAME!" by Tsutomu Nihei. The player controls a character who is trying to escape from a giant labyrinth while avoiding being caught by a monster. This game uses the sound cue to indicate the distance between the player and the collectible items. Players need to avoid the monster and collect all the items to win the game.
