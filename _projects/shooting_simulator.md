This project is done in my last year of undergraduate study for my Bachelor's thesis {% cite li2017effect %}. It is a shooting simulator made with HTML5 and JavaScript. The tank models and screenshots are from the game "War Thunder". The simulator is used in cognitive psychology experiments to study the effect of different types of visual stimuli on the accuracy of distance estimation. 

You can find the simulator [here](https://harviu.github.io/Shooting-Simulator/static/index.html).
